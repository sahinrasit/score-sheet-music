The short version

Score has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. Your sheet music, markings and setlists stay on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your scores, markings, setlists and settings are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. They are included in your own device backups (for example iCloud Backup) under your Apple Account, which we cannot access. Score 1.0 does not sync your files through iCloud or any other service.
  • Imported PDFs, photos and scans are kept in the app's storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Camera and Photos

  • Head-gesture page turns (optional). If you turn this feature on, Score uses the front TrueDepth camera to detect a head turn. The camera data is processed on your device in real time, solely to turn the page. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent to us or anyone else, and the camera is not used for this while the feature is off. Face data: this feature uses Apple’s ARKit face tracking only to measure the angle of your head. Face data is not collected, stored or retained, is never shared with us or any third party, and is not used for advertising, analytics or any other purpose. It never leaves your device.
  • Scanning sheet music. When you choose to scan paper music, the camera is used to capture the pages you scan, which are saved as a PDF in Score on your device.
  • Photos. Score only accesses the images you choose to import.

You can change camera and photo access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Purchases

Score Lifetime is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details or your Apple Account information.

To validate purchases and restore them on your devices, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and your purchase history (such as the product, price, currency, storefront and transaction dates). This ID is not linked to your name, email or identity, and the information is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics

Score contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites. There is no Score server and no Score account.

Children

Score is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from children.
