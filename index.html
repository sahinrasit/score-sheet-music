What Score does

Two pages side by side

On iPad, an unfolded foldable iPhone or iPhone in landscape, Score shows two pages at once, like an open book on a music stand. Half-page turns let you see what comes next early.

Hands-free page turns

Turn pages with a Bluetooth page-turner pedal or keyboard, or with a small turn of your head on Face ID devices.

Apple Pencil markings

Pen, highlighter and stamps for fingerings, dynamics and bowings, on three layers (My marks, Teacher, Conductor) you can show or hide. The original PDF is never modified.

Setlists

Put pieces in order for a rehearsal or concert. Perform mode turns from the last page of one piece straight into the next.

Metronome

Tempo saved per score, tap tempo, accented beats and a visual pulse that works in silent mode.

Private by design

No account, no server, no ads, no analytics. Your music stays on your device.

Frequently asked questions

How do I import my sheet music?

Score reads PDF files. From your library you can import PDFs from the Files app (including iCloud Drive and any cloud storage provider that appears in Files), add photos from your Photos library, or scan paper sheet music with the camera — photos and scans become PDFs. You can also send a PDF from Mail, Messages or AirDrop to Score with Open in Score or the share sheet.

The free version includes up to 3 imported scores and 1 setlist; the six sample scores are always free.

How do I pair a Bluetooth page-turner pedal?

Most page-turner pedals (AirTurn, PageFlip, Donner and similar) work as a Bluetooth keyboard. Put the pedal in pairing mode, then pair it in the iOS Settings → Bluetooth — not inside Score. Score turns the page on arrow keys (← → ↑ ↓), Page Up / Page Down, Space and Enter. If your pedal sends something else, use Learn pedal in Score and press the pedal to map it. If the pedal has several modes, check its manual for the “keyboard” or “arrow keys” mode.

Tip: while a keyboard pedal is connected, iOS may hide the on-screen keyboard. Switch the pedal off when you need to type.

How do head-gesture page turns work?

On iPhone and iPad models with Face ID, Score can use the front TrueDepth camera to notice a small head turn: turn your head right for the next page, left to go back. Everything is processed on your device in real time; nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The feature is optional and the camera is only used while it is on.

How do I restore my purchase?

Score Lifetime is a one-time purchase — no subscription — that unlocks unlimited scores and setlists on all your iPhones and iPads, and it is shared with your family through Family Sharing. On a new or reset device, sign in with the same Apple Account; Restore Purchases is on the paywall and in Settings. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my files and markings stored? Are they synced?

Your scores, markings and setlists are stored on your device and are included in your regular iPhone or iPad backup (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer). Version 1.0 does not sync between devices through iCloud. To move a piece to another device, import the PDF there too; you can also export a marked-up copy of a PDF to share.

Do my markings change the original PDF?

No. Markings are kept on separate layers on top of the page, so the original PDF is never modified. To share your markings, export a marked-up copy of the PDF.
